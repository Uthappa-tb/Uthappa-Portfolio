I manage end-to-end Marketing Automation projects, including:

I prioritize clear communication — surfacing risks early, keeping stakeholders in sync, and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks between planning and delivery. Whether managing one project or many in parallel, I aim to bring consistency and calm to the process.
